PCOS Ally

Your PCOS,
finally in one place.

PCOS Ally tracks the symptoms, cycle, plates and habits that matter with PCOS, explains what the research actually says with an evidence badge on every claim, and builds a one-page summary you can hand your doctor. Private by design: everything stays on your phone.

Track what matters

Symptoms, mood, sleep, period, water, weight, labs. One tap from anywhere, patterns over 30 days.

Eat without dieting

The plate method, 40 recipes, a week plan and a grocery list. No calories, no weight cap.

Understand your PCOS

26 short lessons picked for your phenotype and goal, each graded by evidence.

Local-first

No account, no cloud, no analytics. Your health data never leaves your device.

Support

Questions, bugs, feature requests: email hello@pcosally.app and you'll get a reply from the person behind Ally, usually within a couple of days. If the price is what's stopping you, write too.

Frequently asked

  • Where is my data stored? On your device only. Deleting the app deletes your data; include PCOS Ally in your iCloud device backup if you want a copy.
  • Do I need an account? No. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • How does the subscription work? PCOS Ally is offered as an auto-renewing subscription with a 7-day trial on the yearly plan. Prices, duration and renewal terms are shown in the app before you subscribe.
  • How do I cancel? App Store → your profile → Subscriptions, or the Manage subscription link in PCOS Ally's settings. Cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing.
  • Does PCOS Ally tell me which type of PCOS I have? No. You tell it what your clinician found; the app adapts lessons and suggestions to that, and never diagnoses.
  • Does it connect to Apple Health? Not yet. Everything you track lives inside the app.
