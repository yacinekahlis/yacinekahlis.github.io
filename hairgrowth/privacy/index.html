Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mane (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Mane mobile application (the “App”), published by Yacine Kahlis. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

The short version: Mane has no account, no login, and never asks for your email. Your hair photos, readings, routine and notes are stored on your device. When you take a scan or scan a product, the photos are sent over an encrypted connection to our server and an AI provider for the reading; our server does not store them. No ads, we never sell your data, and we do not track you across other apps.

Your anonymous identifier

The App has no accounts. The first time you open it, it generates a random, anonymous identifier on your device. It is not tied to your name, email or Apple ID. We use it to manage your subscription, to apply fair-use limits to AI features, and for anonymous analytics. Deleting the App deletes the identifier; if you reinstall, the App creates a new one.

Information stored on your device only

  • Hair photos: the photos you take for each weekly scan (three views: back, part and hairline) and the photos of your product packaging.
  • Scan readings: your Hair Score, its indices, and the notes that come with each reading.
  • Profile: your first name (if you give one), your goals, and your hair profile, such as curl pattern and length.
  • Routine and care: your routine, your product shelf, and your care log.
  • Reminders: your reminder settings.

This data lives in the App's local storage on your device. It may be included in the device backups you configure with Apple (iCloud or local backups). Those backups are managed by Apple under your Apple account; we have no access to them.

Photos sent for analysis

When you take a scan (the onboarding scan or a weekly scan) or scan a product, the App sends those photos over an encrypted connection to our server. Our server passes them to an AI provider, which produces the reading, and returns the result to the App. Our server does not store the photos.

For your weekly review, the App also sends text context: your goal, your scores, your routine steps, the kinds of products you own, and events you logged (for example a trim).

The AI provider processes this data under its API terms, which do not allow it to use the content to train its models.

Information kept on our server

  • Your free onboarding reading: for the free onboarding scan only, we keep the text result of that reading (your hair profile, never the photo), linked to your anonymous identifier, so the free scan is counted once and its result can be restored.
  • Usage records: each time the App requests an analysis, we record your anonymous identifier and the time, so we can apply fair-use limits and control costs. These records are kept for a limited period.

Analytics

We use an analytics provider to understand how the App is used. It receives anonymous product-usage events, such as the screens you view and the features you use, tied to your anonymous identifier. It never receives your photos, your name, or text you write.

Permissions

  • Camera and photo library: used only to take or choose your scan photos and product photos.
  • Notifications: local reminders scheduled on your device (a care reminder and a weekly scan reminder). They are not sent through a server.

Third-party services

  • Apple: processes App Store payments. Apple may also collect standard diagnostics (such as crash reports) according to your device settings and Apple's privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat: manages subscriptions. It receives your anonymous identifier and your purchase status. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • An AI provider: produces your readings from the photos and text context described above.
  • A cloud hosting provider: hosts our server.
  • An analytics provider: receives the anonymous usage events described above.

We do not show ads, we do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Data retention and deletion

  • Data on your device stays there until you delete it. Deleting the App removes all locally stored data, including your anonymous identifier.
  • Photos sent for analysis are not stored on our server.
  • Your free onboarding reading is kept on our server, linked to your anonymous identifier, for as long as it is needed for the purposes above or until you ask us to delete it.
  • Usage records are kept for a limited period.

You can ask us to delete the records on our server tied to your anonymous identifier at any time by emailing us. Because the App has no accounts, we will reply with what we need to find your data.

Your rights

Depending on your region (including under GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA and CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Most of your data is on your device, so you can exercise most of these rights directly in the App. For data held on our server, or for anything else, contact us below.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
