Mane

See your hair,
week after week.

Mane is a weekly three-photo hair scan: back, part and hairline. Each scan gives you a Hair Score, and Mane builds a care routine from the products you already own.

Mane is not a medical app. The Hair Score describes how your hair looks in your photos; it is not a diagnosis and does not promise growth. If you are worried about hair loss, see a doctor or a dermatologist.

Support

Questions, bugs, feature requests: email hello.yk.apps@gmail.com and you'll get a reply from the person behind Mane, usually within a couple of days.

Frequently asked

  • How do I cancel my subscription? On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Mane, or tap Manage subscription in Mane's settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date (or before a free trial ends) and you won't be charged again. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • How do I restore my purchase? After reinstalling Mane or moving to a new iPhone, tap Restore purchases in Mane's settings, signed in with the Apple ID you subscribed with.
  • Where are my photos stored? On your iPhone. When you take a scan, the photos are sent over an encrypted connection to our server and an AI provider for the reading, and the result comes back to the app; our server does not keep the photos. Deleting the app deletes them. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
  • The scan says it can't see my hair. Retake the photos with your hair down, fully in the frame, and in good light. Avoid strong light behind you and deep shadows.
