Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tuner (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Tuner mobile application (the “App”), published by Yacine Kahlis. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

  • Device identifier: an anonymous, randomly generated ID stored on your device. The App has no accounts and never asks for your name or email.
  • Photos you submit: the car photos (and optional reference photos) you choose to transform. They are processed transiently to generate your images and are not stored on our servers.
  • Purchase status: your subscription entitlement and generation-credit balance, processed by Apple and RevenueCat.
  • Usage data: basic, non-identifying diagnostics that help us keep the App reliable.

How we use your information

We use your information to provide the App's features: generating AI transformations of your car photos and managing your subscription. Your generation history is stored locally on your device. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

We rely on trusted third parties to operate the App, including cloud hosting, payment processing (Apple and RevenueCat), and AI providers used to generate images. These providers process data only as needed to deliver their service.

Data retention and deletion

Generated images live on your device to power your history; deleting the App deletes them. You can request deletion of the data linked to your device identifier by contacting us.

Your rights

Depending on your region, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, reach out using the contact details below.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
