My generation looks wrong

AI renders are approximations — results vary with photo quality. For best results, use a sharp, well-lit photo of the whole car, taken from a front three-quarter angle, and try again: every render is different.

I ran out of credits

Your subscription grants credits with each purchase and renewal, and unused credits roll over. Need more before your next renewal? Credit packs are available in the app — tap your balance on the Create tab.

Restoring my subscription

Reinstalled or switched phones? Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Your subscription and credit balance follow your App Store account.

Where are my creations?

Your garage is stored on your device. Save your favorite renders to your photo library from the result screen so they're backed up with your photos; deleting the app deletes the garage. The only build we keep on our server is your first one, so it comes back if you reinstall the app.

Managing or cancelling the subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tuner. Refunds are handled by Apple through reportaproblem.apple.com.
