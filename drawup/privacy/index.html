DrawUp

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DrawUp (“we”, “us”) handles your information when you use the DrawUp mobile application (the “App”), published by Yacine Kahlis. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

The short version: DrawUp is local-first. Everything you track — protocols, doses, schedules, notes, injection sites — is stored on your device and never sent to us. There is no account, no analytics, no ads, and we could not sell your data even if we wanted to, because we never receive it.

Information stored on your device only

  • Tracking data: your compounds, protocols, doses, reconstitution inputs, schedules, injection sites, and notes. This data lives in the App's local storage on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any server we operate.
  • Reminders: dose reminders are local notifications scheduled by your device. They are not sent through a server.
  • Preferences: App settings, also stored locally.

Information processed by third parties

  • Purchase status: subscriptions are processed by Apple and by RevenueCat, our subscription-management provider. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated identifier and your entitlement status — never your tracking data, name, or email. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • App Store: Apple may collect standard, aggregated diagnostics (such as crash reports) according to your device settings and Apple's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or share your personal information — we never possess it.
  • We do not run analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We cannot read your protocols, doses, or notes. Ever.

Backups

Because your data lives on your device, it may be included in the device backups you configure with Apple (iCloud or local backups). Those backups are managed and encrypted by Apple under your Apple account; we have no access to them.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Removing an entry in the App deletes it immediately, and deleting the App removes all locally stored data. To ask RevenueCat to delete the anonymous identifier linked to your purchases, contact us and we will pass the request along.

Your rights

Depending on your region (including under GDPR and CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Since your data is on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the App. For anything else, contact us below.

Children

The App is intended for adults and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.
