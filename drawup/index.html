Frequently asked

  • Where is my data stored? On your device only. Deleting the app deletes your data; include DrawUp in your iCloud device backup if you want a copy.
  • Do I need an account? No. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • Is the calculator really free? Yes — unlimited, forever. The subscription covers protocol tracking, reminders and history.
  • How do I cancel my subscription? App Store → your profile → Subscriptions, or the Manage subscription link in DrawUp's settings.
  • Does DrawUp give dosing advice? Never. DrawUp computes and records the numbers you enter — it is not medical advice. Always consult a licensed professional.
