Slabby

What are your old cards worth?

Slabby is a trading card scanner for iPhone. Point your camera at a card: Slabby finds the exact card and its print, with today's market price in your currency. Your cards go into a binder with one total, sorted by value. No account.

Real prices, never guessed. Slabby shows market prices published by others: TCGplayer and Cardmarket through TCGdex for Pokémon, JustTCG for the games in beta. It never makes a price up, and when no source covers a print it shows nothing. Prices are indicative: they are not an appraisal of your card or an offer to buy it.

Frequently asked

  • How does a scan work? Hold your iPhone over a card: Slabby spots it and takes the photo. The photo goes over an encrypted connection to our server, where an AI model reads the card's name, number, set, language and print, and Slabby matches them against the card catalogue. If it picked the wrong one, tap “Not the right card?” to choose from the closest matches, or search by name and number. A photo Slabby can't use (glare, blur, a card cut off) or a card it can't find doesn't use up a scan.
  • Where do the prices come from? For Pokémon cards: the TCGplayer market price (in US dollars) and the Cardmarket trend price (in euros), through TCGdex, refreshed about once a day. Each print has its own price (Normal, Holo, Reverse Holo, 1st Edition…), and a card printed in another language than English is priced from Cardmarket only. Prices are converted to your currency with the European Central Bank's daily reference rates; the card's details always show the original price, its source and the date of the rate. They are prices of raw (ungraded) copies: a worn card usually sells for less.
  • Which games does Slabby read? Pokémon, plus Magic: The Gathering, Yu-Gi-Oh!, One Piece, Disney Lorcana and other games in beta. Beta prices are JustTCG's Near Mint market prices, in US dollars, for English cards for now. Beta cards have no catalogue picture, so Slabby keeps your photo as the card's picture. A beta card is priced only when its number matches the catalogue; otherwise Slabby opens “Search by name” so you can pick it. Beta means incomplete: some cards and prints are missing, and it may change.
  • What are graded prices? Where they are available (in beta, English Pokémon cards only), Slabby shows what copies graded PSA 10, 9, 8 and 7 sell for, from JustTCG's graded market data. Slabby never grades your card and never guesses its grade: the grade is PSA's call. Graded prices come with Slabby Pro and the free trial.
  • How does the free trial work? The weekly plan starts with a 3-day free trial that includes 5 scans with full prices; Slabby can remind you the day before it ends. Cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing. The yearly plan has no free trial, and buying it during the trial ends the trial and charges you right away. Prices are shown in the app before you subscribe.
  • How do I cancel my subscription? In Slabby, open Settings and tap Manage on your plan, or on your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Slabby. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date (or before the free trial ends) and you won't be charged again. Deleting the app, or deleting your data in it, does not cancel a subscription.
  • How do I restore my purchase? After reinstalling Slabby or moving to a new iPhone, tap Restore purchases in Slabby's Settings (or Restore on the plans screen), signed in with the Apple Account you subscribed with.
  • How do I delete my data? In Slabby, open Settings → Your data → Delete all my data. It needs a connection: it erases your binder, your card photos and your settings on this iPhone, and the scan history our server keeps for it, then Slabby starts over as on its first launch. Your subscription is not cancelled; tap Restore to get Pro back. What is erased and what is kept is detailed in the Privacy Policy.
