Card readings

Cards are identified by an AI model from your photo, then matched against card catalogues. The identification can be wrong, especially with glare, blur, an unusual print or a card the catalogues do not have. Check the card, its set, its number and its print (Normal, Holo, Reverse Holo, 1st Edition…) before relying on a price: you can choose another print, pick from the closest matches, or search by name. Slabby does not authenticate cards and cannot tell a genuine card from a counterfeit.

Prices

  • Pokémon prices are the TCGplayer market price and the Cardmarket trend price as published through TCGdex, for raw (ungraded) copies. Our server keeps a card's prices for up to a day before asking TCGdex again, or longer while TCGdex cannot be reached.
